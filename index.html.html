01 — Revenue Assurance · Robi Axiata
CDR Mediation & Revenue Assurance Platform
Protecting $600M+ in annual revenue through proactive monitoring and anomaly detection on 30M+ daily records
Billing · Mediation
The Problem
Robi's billing and mediation stack processes tens of millions of CDRs (Call Detail Records) daily across prepaid, postpaid, roaming, and interconnect domains. The environment was reactive: revenue leakage from mediation gaps, duplicate records, or processing failures was only detected after it had already occurred — sometimes hours later — making root cause analysis slow and financial reconciliation costly. There was no unified monitoring layer spanning all transaction types.
What I Did As PM
I led product vision for the mediation and billing systems, defining a proactive monitoring framework that placed anomaly-detection checkpoints at critical mediation pipeline stages. I worked with engineering to formalise transaction spike alerting, partnered with finance to align revenue assurance KPIs to the product roadmap, and drove structured incident review cycles. I also defined SLA requirements, owned escalation workflows with vendors, and reduced the mean time-to-detection (TTD) for systemic risks through automated threshold-based alerting.

03 — Network Systems · Mar–Jun 2024 · Robi Axiata
Telecom Network Asset Discovery & Reconciliation System Enhancement
Modernising network asset tracking by integrating Ericsson 2G BTS, Transmission, and NCE IP elements into live reconciliation infrastructure
Network · Integration
The Problem
Robi's network asset discovery and reconciliation system lacked adapters for key infrastructure categories — including Ericsson 2G BTS nodes, Ericsson Transmission assets, and NCE IP elements. These gaps meant large portions of the network estate weren't being tracked in real time, reconciliation was incomplete, and asset record quality was degraded by invalid entries. Location changes and newly serialised assets went undetected, creating operational blind spots.
What I Did As PM
I oversaw adapter integration, ensuring seamless compatibility with the existing discovery and reconciliation framework. I collaborated with engineering to optimise the asset data parsing and matching process, and managed the reconciliation workflow — validating and updating asset records against business requirements. I defined the real-time tracking requirements for location changes and new serialised assets, and drove down the occurrence of invalid entries in the system through improved matching logic and record governance.

Call Centre Technology (CCT) Infrastructure Migration
Leading a zero-downtime contact centre infrastructure upgrade across servers, VRRP, and non-voice service connectivity
Migration · Infra
The Problem
Robi's contact centre infrastructure was running on ageing CCT servers that limited scalability, resilience, and integration with modern AI-assisted tools. A full infrastructure migration was required — with no tolerance for service downtime during cutover, as any disruption would directly impact customer service operations and satisfaction at scale.
