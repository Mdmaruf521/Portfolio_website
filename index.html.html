Selected Case Studies

01 — Revenue Systems
Telecom Billing & Mediation Revenue Assurance Platform
Reducing revenue leakage in a multi-system BSS environment at national scale
Billing · Mediation
The Problem
Robi's billing and mediation stack was a complex, multi-vendor environment handling prepaid, postpaid, interconnect, and roaming transactions simultaneously. With millions of CDRs (Call Detail Records) processed daily across multiple mediation nodes, any gap in monitoring or workflow breakdown created silent revenue leakage that was difficult to trace. There was no unified view across billing domains, and incident response relied heavily on reactive escalations rather than proactive detection.
What I Did As PM
I owned the end-to-end product strategy for the mediation and billing systems. I defined and prioritised improvements to system monitoring workflows, partnered with the engineering team to build anomaly detection checkpoints at key mediation stages, and worked with finance to align revenue assurance KPIs into the product roadmap. I drove structured incident review cycles that converted reactive fire-fighting into systematic process improvements — ensuring vendor accountability and reducing the mean time to resolution (MTTR).
10M+ Subscribers served
~40% Faster incident MTTR
Zero Tolerance SLA on billing

02 — Enterprise Infrastructure
Digital Asset Management System (SAP-Integrated)
Centralising enterprise asset lifecycle visibility across a national telecom operator
Platform · SAP
The Problem
Robi manages thousands of physical and digital infrastructure assets — from network equipment and IT hardware to software licenses across departments. These assets were tracked inconsistently across siloed spreadsheets, ERP modules, and manual procurement records. The lack of a single source of truth meant asset utilisation was opaque, procurement decisions were made without full visibility, and audit cycles were painfully manual and error-prone.
What I Did As PM
I led the product strategy for a centralised Digital Asset Management platform built on SAP infrastructure. I worked with IT, procurement, finance, and facilities teams to map existing asset data flows, define a canonical data model, and prioritise features for the initial and subsequent releases. I drove the integration requirements between the DAM platform and existing ERP modules, ensuring asset lifecycle events (procurement → deployment → maintenance → decommission) were captured systematically. I also defined the dashboard requirements that gave operational teams real-time visibility into asset health and utilisation.
1 Source Of truth for assets
Multi-dept Cross-team adoption
Audit-ready Compliance output

03 — Digital Transformation
Enterprise eSign Digital Contract Platform
Eliminating paper-based contract workflows across commercial, legal, and procurement teams
Digital · Workflow
The Problem
Contract execution at Robi was largely paper-driven — multi-party agreements required physical printing, courier routing, and manual archiving. In a commercial environment where vendor contracts, partner agreements, and regulatory documents are time-sensitive, this introduced significant delays, version control risks, and compliance gaps. The process was also impossible to audit efficiently, with contracts scattered across email threads and physical storage systems.
What I Did As PM
I defined the product scope, user flows, and integration requirements for Robi's enterprise eSign platform. I conducted stakeholder interviews with legal, procurement, and commercial teams to understand pain points and map the existing contract lifecycle. I prioritised an MVP that addressed the highest-volume contract types first, then planned phased expansion. I drove integration with enterprise identity systems for signer authentication and worked closely with the legal team to ensure the solution met regulatory requirements for digital signatures in Bangladesh. Post-launch, I tracked adoption metrics and coordinated training rollouts across business units.
~80% Faster signing cycle
Paperless Contract archiving
Multi-team Enterprise rollout
